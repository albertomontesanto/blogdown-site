Overview

GenealogyAI is an innovative software platform for the automated reconstruction of multigenerational genealogical networks from historical birth, marriage, and death records that have been previously digitized and indexed.

The system combines probabilistic record linkage procedures with a genealogical inference framework based on temporal, biological, and relational constraints. This approach enables the automatic identification of individuals appearing in different documents and the consistent reconstruction of their family relationships.

The system's decision parameters are calibrated using manually validated reference genealogies, enabling automatic adaptation to different archival contexts and historical periods.

How GenealogyAI works

01

Historical record integration

Uses indexed birth, marriage, and death records as the basis for reconstructing family histories.

02

Identity and relationship inference

Links records probabilistically and infers family relationships under temporal, biological, and relational constraints.

03

Reference-based calibration

Calibrates decision parameters against manually validated genealogies to accommodate different historical datasets.

Purpose: To support the consistent reconstruction of historical family networks across generations, while adapting the analysis to the characteristics of individual archival collections.
